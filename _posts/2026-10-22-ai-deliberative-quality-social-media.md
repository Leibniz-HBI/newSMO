The study evaluates open-weight language models on aspects of deliberation such as civility, reciprocity, emotion, and storytelling. The authors use the [Database of Public Speakers]({{ '/dbos/' | relative_url }}) to identify public speakers and compare how they and other users participate in German-language climate debates on X.
